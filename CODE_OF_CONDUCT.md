# HeyTim community code of conduct

HeyTim is building software for people to collaborate with AI teammates. We want
the project’s public spaces to be useful, welcoming, and safe for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Keep feedback focused on the product, code, documentation, or proposal.
- Protect private information, credentials, customer data, and security details.
- Credit other people’s work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, community discussions,
project events, and other spaces where someone is representing HeyTim.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@heytim.ai](mailto:support@heytim.ai?subject=HeyTim%20conduct%20report).
Include links or screenshots when they are safe to share. Do not post personal
information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or
lock discussions, restrict participation, or ban an account when needed to
protect the community. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical.
